# Cascade

A quiet table, a shuffled deck, and a little possibility. Cascade is Klondike solitaire in Draw Three mode, with warm ivory cards on deep teal felt.

Build the four foundations from Ace to King. Arrange the columns in descending ranks and alternating colors, uncover the cards beneath, and save an empty column for a King. Turn three cards from the stock at a time and play the front card; when the deck runs out, cycle through again.

Drag a single card or carry a whole run across the table. A warm outline shows where your cards can land. Double-click or double-tap an exposed card to send it home. There is no ticking clock: take the time you need.

Bring the last King home and the orderly table gives way. Cards leap from the foundations, tumble under gravity, bounce across the floor, and leave a trail of overlapping faces until the felt disappears beneath them. Touch the table to begin again.
